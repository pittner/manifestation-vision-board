12 questions before you choose what to manifest

Answer them alone, with a notebook, in 15 minutes. Be honest, nobody else reads it. The answers that surprise you point to the goals that will actually stick.

  1. What do I want so much that I have stopped saying it out loud?
  2. What would I go after if I knew it would work?
  3. Which of my goals are really mine, and which belong to someone else?
  4. How do I want to feel on an ordinary Tuesday in December 2027?
  5. What did I already make happen in 2026 that I once only wished for?
  6. Which belief about money, love or work holds me back the most?
  7. Who already lives the life I want, and what do they do every day?
  8. What am I willing to give up to get what I want?
  9. What would make 2027 my best year so far?
  10. Which goal scares me a little, in a good way?
  11. What small action could I take every day for the next 30 days?
  12. If my vision came true, what is the first thing I would do?

Make your manifestation board in 45 minutes

  1. 10 minutes: write what you want. No limits and no editing. Money, love, work, health, home and how you want to feel.
  2. 15 minutes: answer the questions. Go through the 12 questions below. Short, honest answers are enough; nobody else reads them.
  3. 10 minutes: pick three goals. Not ten. Choose the three that matter most and write each one in the present tense, as if it is already true.
  4. 10 minutes: make it visible. Turn your main goal and its first step into a phone lock screen, so you see it every day and act on it, not only wish for it.

Questions

What should I put on a manifestation vision board?

Put the goal in your own words, written as if it is already true, a first step you can do this week, and your reason why. This page has 36 goals in 6 groups (money and abundance, love and relationships, career and success, health and energy, home and lifestyle, mindset and inner peace), each with a first step, so you can pick three and make them visible.

Does a manifestation vision board actually work?

A board does not make things happen by itself. What helps is that you see the goal every day, so you notice chances and choose actions that move you toward it. That is why every goal here comes with a concrete first step, and the habit that works best is putting it on your phone lock screen.

How do I manifest with a vision board?

Pick a few clear goals, write them in the present tense, add one action for this week, and put the board where you see it every day. Review it each week and update the next step.

How many goals should I put on my vision board for 2027?

Three is enough: one big goal, one about how you want to feel, and one small daily habit. Fewer goals get done more often.
